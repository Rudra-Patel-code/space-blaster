🚀 How to Play

🎯 Objective

Survive as long as you can while shooting down enemies and collecting power-ups to increase your score!

🎮 Controls

  • W, A, S, D — Move your spaceship
  • Left Click — Shoot light weapon (free)
  • Right Click — Shoot heavy weapon (uses ammo)
  • Spacebar — Activate Lazer Beam (has cooldown)

🔋 Power-ups

  • Health — Restores health to full
  • Shield — Makes you invincible for 20 seconds
  • Bullets — Adds 3 heavy bullets
